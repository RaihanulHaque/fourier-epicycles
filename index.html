The outline is sampled at 1024 points. A discrete Fourier transform turns the x values into coefficients ak, bk and the y values into ck, dk.

Each term akcos kt + bksin kt is one circle of radius √(ak²+bk²) spinning k times per loop. Stacking them tip to tail adds the terms.

top chain gives x, left chain gives y. In one-chain mode, the points are complex numbers z = x + iy and a single chain of circles draws the shape directly.
